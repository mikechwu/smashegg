# STATUS

**This file moved to [`status/`](status/) on 2026-07-28.** It had grown to 7,895 lines of
reverse-chronological rounds, and a reader needed the top one or two.

**Start here:**

- **[`status/CURRENT.md`](status/CURRENT.md)** — what is decided, what is open, what blocks
  what. One page, always true.
- **[`status/README.md`](status/README.md)** — which two files to open for which question.

The rest: [`status/MODEL.md`](status/MODEL.md) (every constant and formula, generated from
`status/model.json`), [`status/VALIDATED.md`](status/VALIDATED.md) (measured or modelled,
n, configuration, range), [`status/WITHDRAWN.md`](status/WITHDRAWN.md) (retracted figures),
and [`status/rounds/`](status/rounds/INDEX.md) (the 118 former sections, moved verbatim and
never edited).
